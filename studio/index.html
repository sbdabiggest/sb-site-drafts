FAQ

What do I send?

Your vocal tracks as WAV (lead, doubles, ad-libs — dry, no effects, from the start of the song), the beat as WAV or high-quality MP3, and one reference song you want it to feel like. Zip them, upload to Drive/WeTransfer/Dropbox, paste the link at checkout.

How fast?

Mix + master: 48 hours from when your files arrive. Same-day is a separate option (files in before 2pm Central). Tuning and mastering alone: 24 hours.

Do I own the result?

Yes. You own your song and the delivered files. I keep the right to use short before/after snippets as examples unless you ask me not to on the order.

Revisions and refunds?

Mixes include two revision rounds; tuning and masters include one. If I can’t make it right, the order is refunded before delivery. After delivery, sales are final.
