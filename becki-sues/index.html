Good to know

Questions

✦
Do you really come to me?

Yes — that’s the whole idea. Driveway, office car park, marina slip, campsite, or storage lot. I bring my own water and power, so you don’t need to provide anything.

How long does it take?

A full interior on a car or SUV is usually two to three hours. Exterior work is one to two. Boats and RVs depend on length and how much oxidation there is — I’ll tell you the honest number when I quote it.

How do I get an exact price?

Text me a few photos, inside and out. I’ll come back with a firm price and a time I can be there. The price is agreed before I start, so the invoice never surprises you.

What do you take for payment?

Cash, Zelle, Cash App, or card. Payment is due when the job is finished, after we’ve walked around it together.

Can you get out pet hair, stains, and smells?

Usually, yes. Pet hair and set-in stains take extra time, so mention them when you send photos and I’ll build it into the quote rather than surprising you on the day.

What if the weather turns?

I’ll call you the day before if it looks bad and we’ll move it. I would rather reschedule than rush a job in the rain and hand back work I’m not proud of.
