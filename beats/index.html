FAQ

Can I use a beat for free?

Yes, for non-profit use only: SoundCloud, YouTube without monetization, freestyles, demos. Credit “(prod. Soberbby)” in the title. The free file keeps the voice tag. To put a song on Spotify, Apple Music or a monetized channel, you need a license.

What do I get after I pay?

An instant download page with the untagged files for your tier plus the license agreement PDF with your name, the beat, and the order number. Links stay live for an hour; the email receipt from Stripe has the order id if you need them re-issued.

Is a lease exclusive?

No. Leases are non-exclusive — the same beat can be licensed to other artists. Exclusive rights remove the beat from the store and transfer it to you; message @SBdabiggest for that.

Refunds?

Digital files can’t be returned, so all sales are final. If a download fails, message @SBdabiggest with your order id and it gets fixed the same day.

Who makes these?

Soberbby — an artist and engineer from Destin, Florida who records, mixes and masters in this lane. Every beat here is the kind of beat he would write to himself.
