How much does lawn service cost in Mary Esther, FL?

Per-cut and monthly prices for typical Mary Esther and Fort Walton Beach yards, and the four things that move the number.

For a typical Mary Esther lot (a quarter acre or less), regular mowing with edging and blowing runs about $45–$65 per cut, or roughly $160–$240 a month on a weekly schedule in season. Bigger lots, thick St. Augustine, fences and heavy edging push it up; a bare, flat yard on a bi-weekly plan brings it down.

"What do you charge?" is the first thing every homeowner asks, and most lawn companies make you wait for a quote to find out. Here is the straight answer for our part of the Emerald Coast, with the reasons a yard lands at the low or high end.

Typical prices around Mary Esther and Fort Walton Beach

  • Small lot (under a quarter acre), weekly: $45–$55 per cut. Mow, string-trim, edge the drive and walk, blow off.
  • Standard lot (quarter to half acre), weekly: $55–$75 per cut.
  • Bi-weekly plans: usually $10–$15 more per cut than weekly, because there is twice the grass to deal with, but fewer visits a month.
  • One-time cut or overgrown yard: $90–$200 depending on height. Grass over a foot tall is a cleanup job, not a mow.
  • Extras: hedge trimming, bed weeding, mulch and cleanups are quoted per job; most run $75–$300.

Those are working ranges for 2026, not a promise for your exact yard. A five-minute look, or two photos by text, gets you a firm number.

The four things that change the price

  1. Size and shape. Square, open lots are fast. Narrow side yards, islands, and lots of obstacles add time.
  2. Grass type and growth. St. Augustine in July after rain grows like it's being paid to. Thick growth means slower mowing and more trimming.
  3. Edging and detail. Long driveways, sidewalks, fence lines and bed edges are where the real time goes. A yard with a lot of edge costs more than a bigger yard with none.
  4. Schedule. Weekly in season is cheaper per cut and keeps the yard looking kept. Skipping weeks costs more per visit and the yard never quite gets ahead.

What you're actually paying for

A reliable crew that shows up on the same day every week, with sharp blades, insurance, and fuel money, and leaves the drive blown off. The cheapest cut in town is usually the one that stops showing up in August. If a quote is far under the ranges above, ask who is insured and what happens the week it rains.

Frequently asked questions

Do you charge more in summer?

The per-cut price stays the same. What changes is how often the yard needs it — most Mary Esther yards need weekly from May through September and can stretch to every other week from November to February.

Is there a contract?

No contract for regular mowing. We agree on a schedule and you can pause or cancel by text. Cleanups and landscaping are quoted per job with the price agreed before we start.

Can I get a price without a visit?

Usually yes. Text your address and two photos of the yard and you'll get a firm per-cut price the same day. Corner lots and heavy overgrowth get an in-person look.
