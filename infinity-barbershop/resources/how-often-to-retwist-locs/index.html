How often should you retwist your locs?

Starter, mature and interlocked locs each have their own schedule; here it is, plus the at-home routine that stretches it.

Starter locs: every 4–6 weeks. Mature locs: every 6–8 weeks, or when the new growth is about an inch. Interlocked locs can go 8–12 weeks. Retwisting more often than that thins the roots over time; in Gulf Coast humidity the trick is a light routine at home, not more twisting.

The most common question at the chair, and the most common mistake is the same: retwisting too often because the roots look fuzzy. Fuzz is normal. Thin roots are not. Here is the schedule that keeps locs strong.

By stage

  • Starter locs (first 3–6 months): every 4–6 weeks. They need the pattern set, but frequent twisting on baby locs is how people end up with weak spots.
  • Mature locs: every 6–8 weeks. New growth of about an inch is the signal.
  • Interlocked locs: 8–12 weeks. The knot at the root holds longer, especially through humidity and workouts.
  • Between visits: a wash, a light oil at the scalp, and a tie-down at night. That's it.

Why over-twisting hurts

Every retwist puts tension at the root. Do it every two weeks and the loc gets thinner where it meets the scalp, which is where breakage starts. If the roots look fuzzy at week three, that's growth doing its job. Palm-roll lightly after a wash if it bothers you; save the real retwist for the schedule.

Florida humidity

Humidity swells hair and loosens a fresh twist faster than dry climates. It does not mean twist more; it means: dry locs fully after washing (a hooded dryer or time in the sun), use a light hold product instead of heavy gels that flake and mold, and cover at night with satin. A retwist that's set and dried properly lasts the full six to eight weeks here.

Frequently asked questions

How long does a retwist take?

Roughly 60 to 90 minutes for a full head, longer with a wash and style. Book the time on Fresha or Booksy so the chair is yours.

Retwist or interlock?

Retwist keeps a rounder, softer loc and needs more upkeep; interlocking holds longer and handles humidity and sweat better. We'll look at your hair and tell you straight.

Can I wash my locs between retwists?

Yes, and you should, every one to two weeks. Clean locs lock faster and smell better. Just dry them completely.
