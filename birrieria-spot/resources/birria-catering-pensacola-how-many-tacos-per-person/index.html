Birria for a party: how many tacos per person?

The per-person math for tacos and consomé, what travels, and how to set up a catering order in Pensacola.

Plan on 3–4 quesabirria tacos per adult and one cup of consomé per 2–3 tacos. For 20 people that's about 70 tacos and 25 cups of consomé. Tacos hold well for an hour in foil pans; keep the consomé hot separately and let people dip at the table.

Birria is a party food: it comes out of one pot, it feeds a crowd, and everybody wants a photo. Here is how to size the order so the tacos don't run out before the consomé does.

Per person

  • Quesabirria tacos: 3 per adult for a mixed meal, 4 if it's the main event. Kids: 2.
  • Consomé: a cup per 2–3 tacos. People go back for it.
  • Extras: lime, onion, cilantro and salsa on the side, always. A tray of chips with the consomé as a dip is the cheapest crowd-pleaser on the table.

For 20 people: about 70 tacos, 25 cups of consomé, salsa on the side. For 50: 175 tacos and the big pots.

What holds

Tacos griddled and packed in foil pans stay crisp enough for an hour; after that they soften but still eat well. Consomé holds hot in a cambro or a slow cooker on warm for hours. Birria ramen and pizza don't travel; those are truck-side orders.

How to book

  1. Message or call with the date, headcount and the address.
  2. Pickup orders are ready at the truck on Mobile Hwy; larger events can be set up as a truck visit or a drop-off around Pensacola.
  3. Two days' notice for pickup, a week for a truck visit or anything over 50.

Frequently asked questions

Do you bring the truck to events?

Yes for larger events around Pensacola. Message the date and headcount and you'll get a quote and the minimum.

Can I get the meat and tortillas separately to make tacos at the party?

Yes: meat, consomé, cheese and tortillas by the pan, and you griddle on site. Great for a backyard setup.

How far in advance?

Two days for a pickup order, a week for a truck visit or big orders.
