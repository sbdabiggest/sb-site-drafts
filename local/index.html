FAQ

Is this an agency?

No. It is one system, built by a detailer for his own business and run by software plus one person (SB). You text SB directly. There is no account manager and no 12-month contract.

Who writes the articles?

They are drafted by the same AI-assisted engine that writes SB Detailing's guides, from questions you give us or that customers ask, and read by a person before they go up. They are honest and local, or they don't publish.

Do I need a website?

For the Reviews plan, no: the review and referral pages can live on a page we host for you. For Growth, the guides need a home, so add the $250 one-page site if you don't have one. You own it.

What do you need from me?

Your Google review link (we'll find it if you don't know it), your logo if you have one, your phone number, and five minutes a month to tell us what customers asked. That's it.

Can I cancel?

Any time, from the link in your Stripe receipt. Everything published stays yours.
