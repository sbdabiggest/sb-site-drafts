How often should you pressure wash a house in Florida humidity?

A realistic maintenance schedule for siding, roofs, driveways and pool cages on the Emerald Coast, and the difference between soft wash and pressure wash.

Around Navarre, a house needs a wash about once a year, the shaded north side sometimes twice; driveways and pool cages once a year; roofs every 2–3 years with a proper soft wash. Wait until it's green and you're paying for a restoration, not a cleaning.

Humidity, salt air and shade turn every surface on the Gulf Coast into a growing medium. The question isn't whether the green comes back, it's when. Here is the schedule that keeps a house looking new for the least money.

The schedule

  • Siding (vinyl, stucco, Hardie, brick): yearly. North and east faces and anything under trees may need a mid-year touch.
  • Roof: every 2–3 years, soft wash only. Black streaks are algae eating the shingle; left alone it shortens roof life.
  • Driveway, walks, patio: yearly, or when the concrete looks grey-black instead of light grey.
  • Pool deck and screen enclosure: yearly, ideally before the season you use it.
  • Fences and decks: every 1–2 years, low pressure on wood.

Soft wash vs pressure wash

Soft washing uses a cleaning solution that kills the mold and algae at the root, then a low-pressure rinse. It's what siding and roofs need, and the result lasts longer because the growth is dead, not just knocked off.

Pressure washing is for hard surfaces: concrete, pavers, stone. Used on siding or a roof it strips paint, forces water into the wall and voids warranties.

A good company uses both, on the right surfaces. If someone quotes "pressure washing the roof," walk away.

Signs it's time

  • Green or black film on the shady side of the house.
  • Dark streaks running down the roof from the ridge.
  • Concrete that stays dark after it dries.
  • Slippery pool deck. That's algae, and it's a fall waiting to happen.

Frequently asked questions

Does washing hurt my plants?

Not when it's done right. Plants get pre-wet and rinsed, and the mix is applied at the strength the surface needs, not blasted.

Best time of year?

Any time it's not pouring. Spring before pollen season and fall after the summer mold bloom are the two most popular.

Can I do it myself with a rental washer?

Concrete, yes, with care. Siding and roofs, we'd rather you didn't: that's where the damage happens.
