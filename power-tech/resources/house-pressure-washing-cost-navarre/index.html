How much does it cost to pressure wash a house in Navarre?

Working price ranges for houses, driveways, pool enclosures and roofs on the Emerald Coast, and the four things that move them.

Around Navarre and Gulf Breeze a full exterior house wash runs about $175–$375 for a typical single-story home, driveways and walks $100–$225, pool decks and screen enclosures $150–$350, and a roof soft-wash $300–$650. Two-story homes, heavy mold, and big lots push the number up; bundling two or three services in one visit brings the per-item price down.

The green streaks on the north side of the house, the black driveway, the pool cage that used to be white: everybody in Navarre gets the same three problems, and everybody asks the same first question. Here are honest ranges for our area and what changes them.

Typical 2026 ranges on the Emerald Coast

  • House wash (single story, up to ~2,000 sq ft): $175–$375. Soft wash with the right mix for mold and algae, rinsed clean, windows and doors included.
  • Two-story: $300–$550.
  • Driveway + sidewalks: $100–$225 depending on length and how black it's gotten.
  • Pool deck + screen enclosure: $150–$350. Enclosures are all edges and screen, which is what takes the time.
  • Roof soft-wash (shingle or tile): $300–$650. Never high pressure on a roof.
  • Bundle: house + driveway + enclosure in one trip usually lands $400–$700 total.

These are working ranges, not a quote. Two photos and your address by message gets you a firm number the same day.

What moves the price

  1. Square footage and stories. More surface, more time, more product.
  2. How bad it is. A yearly maintenance wash is fast. Five years of Gulf Coast mold takes dwell time and a second pass.
  3. Surface type. Vinyl and stucco soft-wash quickly; brick, pavers and rough concrete take longer.
  4. Access. Fences, landscaping right against the house, boats and vehicles that need moving.

Why the lowest quote is the one to question

Soft washing done right uses the correct cleaning mix, plant protection, and low pressure on siding and roofs. A rock-bottom quote usually means high pressure everywhere, which strips paint, forces water behind siding and voids roof warranties. Ask what pressure they use on the roof; the right answer is "none."

Frequently asked questions

How long does a house wash take?

Most single-story homes take 1.5 to 3 hours including the driveway. Two-story and heavy mold jobs are a half day.

Do I need to be home?

No. We need water access, windows closed, and vehicles moved off the driveway. Payment after you've seen it.

Is there a military discount?

Yes, on every service, for active duty and veterans. Mention it when you book.
