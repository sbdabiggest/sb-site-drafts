Mobile mechanic vs taking it to a shop: what does it cost?

Honest cost comparison for the repairs a mobile mechanic does in your driveway, and the jobs that still belong in a shop.

A mobile mechanic's labor rate around Fort Walton Beach is usually the same or a little lower than a shop's; the real savings are the $100–$200 tow you don't pay, no shop day lost, and no upsell walk. Batteries, starters, alternators, brakes, belts, sensors and diagnostics are driveway jobs. Transmissions, engine internals and anything needing a lift are shop jobs, and an honest mobile mechanic tells you which is which before charging you.

The car is dead in the driveway or the brakes are grinding, and the question is whether to get it towed to a shop or have someone come out. Here is how the money actually compares, and what a mobile mechanic can and cannot do on the spot.

Where the money goes

  • Tow: $100–$200 in Okaloosa County for a local hook. A mobile mechanic makes that line item disappear.
  • Labor: mobile rates run about the same as independent shops, sometimes lower because there's no building to pay for. Expect a trip or diagnostic fee that gets applied to the repair.
  • Parts: same parts, same suppliers. Ask for the part number and you can price it yourself.
  • Time: no ride to arrange, no day without the car. For a working person that's real money.

Driveway jobs

  • Won't-start diagnosis: battery, starter, alternator, ignition
  • Brake pads and rotors, calipers
  • Belts, hoses, thermostats, water pumps on most engines
  • Sensors, coils, spark plugs, check-engine diagnosis
  • Oil, filters, fluids
  • Pre-purchase inspections at the seller's house before you buy a used car

Shop jobs (and we'll say so)

  • Transmission rebuilds and internal engine work
  • Anything needing a lift or a press: some suspension, exhaust, subframe work
  • Alignment
  • A/C work needing recovery equipment

Frequently asked questions

Do you charge to come out?

There's a trip/diagnostic fee that's applied to the repair if you go ahead. You'll know the number before anyone drives to you.

Can you work at my job or an apartment complex?

Usually yes, anywhere with safe, flat parking. Some complexes have rules; a quick heads-up to the office handles it.

How fast can you come?

Same or next day for most calls around Fort Walton Beach, Mary Esther and Destin. Say if the car is blocking a driveway or you're stranded.
